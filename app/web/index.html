Setupdrop miner, Steam, Discord

1Drop minerRequired

How it works
  1. Press Install miner. Loot Radar downloads Twitch Drops Miner from its official GitHub and starts it.
  2. Press Log the miner in to Twitch and sign in.
  3. Link your game accounts on twitch.tv/drops/campaigns. Drops only count for games whose account is linked.
—
You sign in once in Loot Radar's window; it passes the login to the miner. Muted mode uses the same sign-in. Open miner dashboard
Until you pick games (Library or Browse drops), the miner mines any drop your linked accounts can get.

2SteamOptional

Loads your Steam games so Loot Radar can pick out drops for games you own. Without it, add games from Browse drops.
How to get these
  1. Open steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey and sign in.
  2. Domain name: type localhost, tick the box, click Register once.
  3. 📱 Now approve it in the Steam app on your phone. Steam doesn't make the key until you do. Open the Steam app, tap the 🔔 notifications bell and approve the API key request. Don't click Register again while you wait: every click sends another request. If you see more than one, approve the newest.
  4. Back on the Steam page, copy the key into the box below.
  5. Your Steam profile's Game details privacy must be Public.

3DiscordOptional

Pings a Discord channel about new drops, Steam news and claimed rewards. Loot Radar works fine without it.
How to get this
  1. Hover the channel you want alerts in and click ⚙️ (Edit Channel).
  2. Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook.
  3. Name it "Loot Radar", then Copy Webhook URL.
Game dealswishlist discounts on Steam and key sites
Adds the cheapest key-shop price across dozens of key shops, and its lowest ever. To get one: sign in on gg.deals, then follow "get your API key" on gg.deals/api. Without it, only Steam prices are checked.
Make a channel for deals, then: ⚙️ Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy Webhook URL. Leave empty to use your main Loot Radar channel.
It also pings when a game drops to its lowest price ever.
Which country's prices GG.deals compares (it has no South Africa option, so its prices show in that region's currency). Steam prices always show in your Steam currency.
Adds an estimate under each GG.deals price (like "≈ R 165"), using the latest exchange rate, updated every 6 hours. It's a guide: the shop's own price and your bank's fees decide what you really pay.
